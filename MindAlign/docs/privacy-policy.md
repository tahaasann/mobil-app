# MindAlign Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-02

MindAlign ("we", "the app") helps you build habits with an AI coach. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## Data we collect

- **Account:** Firebase Authentication user ID; email if you register with email.
- **Habit data:** habits, streak logs, profile settings stored locally on your device.
- **Cloud sync (signed-in users):** bookmarks and chat messages synced to your private Firestore space.
- **AI coach:** excuse text and coach responses processed via Google Gemini through Firebase AI. Do not enter medical emergencies or unrelated content.
- **Subscriptions:** purchase status via RevenueCat (no full payment card data in the app).
- **Analytics & crash reports:** Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for app stability (aggregated).

## How we use data

- Provide coaching, sync, notifications, and subscription features.
- Improve reliability and safety (App Check, abuse reporting for AI responses).

## Your choices

- Use guest mode to keep habit data on-device only.
- Export or delete local data from Profile.
- Registered users: delete account removes cloud sync data per our data deletion flow.

## Third parties

- Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, AI)
- RevenueCat (subscriptions)
- Google Play / App Store (payments)
